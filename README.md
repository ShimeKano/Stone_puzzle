# 🪨 Stone Puzzle

> **Can you turn every stone white?**

A simple puzzle game where every click changes the color of the selected stone and the stones around it.

## 🎮 Play Now

# 👉 [PLAY STONE PUZZLE](https://shimekano.github.io/Stone_puzzle/)

**No installation. No download. Just open and play.**

---

## 🧩 How to Play

* Click a stone to change its state.
* The stone you click and the stones directly around it will change.
* Your goal is simple:

### **Turn every stone white.**

Think carefully. Every move changes the board.

---

## 📈 Progression

The game contains **26 levels**.

| Levels | Board |
| ------ | ----- |
| 1–5    | 3 × 3 |
| 6–10   | 5 × 5 |
| 11–17  | 7 × 7 |
| 18–26  | 9 × 9 |

The puzzles gradually become more challenging as the board grows.

---

## 🏆 Challenge

Try to:

* 🧠 Solve puzzles with fewer moves
* ⏱️ Beat your best time
* 🔥 Reach Level 26
* 👑 Complete the entire game

Every puzzle is generated so that it has a solution.
